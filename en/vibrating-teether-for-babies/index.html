What should parents consider when choosing a teether?

Choose an option that fits your baby and that you can use according to its instructions. Check how it is cleaned, what parts are included, and whether it requires batteries. With any teether, supervision matters: stay with your baby while it is in use and put it away afterward.

For general teething safety, avoid amber or other teething necklaces because of strangulation and choking risks. Avoid teethers that are frozen solid or filled with liquid. Do not use teething gels or medications unless a pediatrician has advised you to do so. If you have a concern about your baby’s teething or comfort, ask a pediatrician.
